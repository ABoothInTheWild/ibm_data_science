## Overview

This module is your **first hands-on data tool**: the spreadsheet. You work in **Excel for the web** from the ground up—entering and formatting data, writing **formulas and functions**, **cleaning** messy data, **filtering and sorting**, and building **pivot tables**. It's a lab-heavy module: **seven guided hands-on labs** carry the bulk of the learning.

The core takeaway:

> Spreadsheets are where most analysts first touch real data. This module builds the **Excel fluency**—formulas, lookups, cleaning, and pivots—you'll rely on before (and alongside) Python and SQL.

---

## Why Excel Matters

Excel is everywhere in business, and for good reason:

* **Low barrier, high reach** — Stakeholders already have it open; results land where people work
* **Fast exploration** — Sort, filter, and pivot to understand a dataset in minutes
* **Cleaning and quality** — Find duplicates, fix inconsistencies, and prepare data before deeper analysis

Building this skill lets you:

* Turn a raw export into a readable, analyzable table
* Answer questions with formulas, lookups, and pivot tables instead of manual counting
* Catch the data-quality problems that would otherwise produce confidently wrong conclusions

This module answers a practical question: *Given a spreadsheet of data, how do I actually work with it?*

## What This Module Covers

The labs use **Excel for the web** (free, available to everyone). The instruction videos use full Excel Desktop, but the labs are written for the web version. The concepts span:

* **Spreadsheet foundations** — terminology, the ribbon, navigating a worksheet, and selecting data
* **Entering and formatting** — typing and editing data, freezing panes, copying/filling (Fill Handle), and auto-fitting columns
* **Formulas and functions** — relative vs. absolute vs. mixed references; `SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `COUNT`, `MIN`, `MAX`; conditional functions (`IF`, nested `IF`, `COUNTIF`, `SUMIF`, `SUMIFS`); and lookups (`VLOOKUP`, `HLOOKUP`)
* **Data cleaning and quality** — removing duplicates/empty rows, fixing inconsistencies (`PROPER`/`UPPER`/`LOWER`, date formats, `TRIM`), Flash Fill, and text functions (`LEFT`/`RIGHT`/`LEN`/`SEARCH`)
* **Filtering and sorting** — AutoFilters, custom and number filters (Top 10), and custom sorts
* **Pivot tables** — formatting data as a table, building pivots, summarizing (Sum/Count/Average), and using Recommended Charts, Filters, Slicers, and Timelines
* **Data privacy and ethics** — the basics of data quality and privacy

## Key Takeaways

* **Spreadsheet fundamentals** — entering, editing, formatting, and navigating data is the base everything else builds on.
* **Formulas and functions** — references plus `SUM`/`AVERAGE`/`COUNT`, conditional functions (`IF`, `COUNTIF`, `SUMIF`), and **VLOOKUP/HLOOKUP** turn a sheet into an analysis engine.
* **Data cleaning** — dedup, case/format fixes, `TRIM`, and Flash Fill are what keep your conclusions trustworthy; this is data quality in practice.
* **Filtering and sorting** let you narrow quickly to what matters.
* **Pivot tables** are the fastest way to summarize and slice a dataset without code—plus slicers and timelines for interactivity.
* **Excel for the web** is the lab environment, so the skills are reproducible by anyone with a free Microsoft account.

## The Data Analyst's Spreadsheet Toolkit

Think of this module as building your **spreadsheet toolkit**, not as memorizing menus.

You don't need to know every Excel function. The goal is to **load a dataset, clean and shape it, and answer real questions with formulas, lookups, and pivot tables**—a workflow that transfers directly to the Python, SQL, and BI tools in the modules ahead.
